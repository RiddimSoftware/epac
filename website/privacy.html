Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

epac is a Canadian civic engagement app built by Riddim Software. This policy explains what data epac collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

No Account Required

epac does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or log in. You can use the full app without identifying yourself in any way.

Data We Do Not Collect

epac does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal information
  • Your location or postal code (the postal code you enter to find your MP stays on your device only)
  • Your browsing or reading history within the app
  • Any analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry sent to Riddim Software

Data Stored on Your Device

epac stores the following data locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Parliamentary data downloaded from official sources (Hansard debates, voting records, MP profiles, expenditures)
  • Your preferences: followed MPs, followed bills, followed topics, your saved postal code
  • Notification preferences you configure

This data never leaves your device to Riddim Software's servers. It is not backed up to iCloud unless you have iCloud backup enabled for all apps.

Network Requests

epac makes network requests to official Canadian government sources to download parliamentary data:

  • Parliament of Canada (ourcommons.ca, api.open.ourcommons.ca) — Hansard, voting records, MP profiles, bills, expenditures
  • Elections Canada (elections.ca) — Electoral data
  • Commissioner of Lobbying (lobbycanada.gc.ca) — Lobbying registry
  • Statistics Canada (statcan.gc.ca) — Census, CPI, and EI data
  • Canada Energy Regulator (CER) — Energy production data
  • IRCC — Immigration statistics
  • Health Canada — Public health notices
  • represent.opennorth.ca — Postal code to riding lookup (used only in the App Clip)

These requests are made directly from your device to the government's servers. Riddim Software does not operate a proxy or intermediary server.

Apple Platform Services

epac uses standard iOS frameworks. Apple may collect data according to its own privacy policy when you use iOS features such as push notifications, Siri, or Spotlight search integration.

No Third-Party Advertising or Analytics

epac contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs (such as Firebase, Amplitude, or Mixpanel), and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Newsletter

If you sign up for Parliament Monthly on the website, your email address is used only to send that monthly digest and related confirmation or unsubscribe messages. Newsletter subscriptions are separate from the epac app, and you can unsubscribe at any time from the link in each email.

Children

epac is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and post the updated policy at this URL.
