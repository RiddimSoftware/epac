Frequently Asked Questions

Is epac free? +

Yes. epac is completely free with no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or hidden costs.

Does epac need an account? +

No. epac requires no login, no email address, and no account of any kind. Your followed MPs, bills, and topics stay on your device.

Where does the data come from? +

All data comes exclusively from official Canadian government sources: Parliament of Canada (Hansard, votes, bills, expenditures), Elections Canada, Statistics Canada, and the Commissioner of Lobbying. No news sources, no AI-generated content.

How often is the data updated? +

Hansard debates are updated the day after each sitting. Voting records are updated within hours of each vote. MP expenditure data is updated quarterly when Parliament publishes it.

Does epac work in French? +

Yes. epac is fully localized in English and Canadian French. Switch your iPhone language to French and the entire app follows.
