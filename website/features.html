Everything epac does

All data comes exclusively from official Canadian government sources — Parliament of Canada, Elections Canada, Statistics Canada, and the Commissioner of Lobbying.

💬 Hansard Debates

Every word spoken in the House of Commons displayed as a group chat — exactly who said what, in order. Tap any speaker to see their full profile. Search debates by topic across all sitting days you've opened.

✅ MP Voting Records

See every recorded vote your MP has cast — Yea, Nay, or Paired — with the final result and date. Filter by bill. Write directly to your MP about any vote with a pre-filled email template.

💰 MP Expenditures

Quarterly travel, hospitality, and contract spending for every Member of Parliament. Drill down into individual travel claims, hospitality events, and supplier contracts.

📋 Bill Tracker

Follow any federal bill from first reading to Royal Assent. Get notified when a bill's stage changes. See PBO independent cost estimates alongside government estimates for major bills.

🏛️ Committee Transcripts

Standing committee hearings from the OurCommons open API — the meetings where MPs question witnesses and study legislation in detail. Speaker attribution distinguishes MPs from expert witnesses.

🗺️ My MP

Enter your postal code to identify your Member of Parliament. See a unified activity feed of their votes, speeches, and expenses in one place. Follow additional MPs to expand your feed.

🏢 Ontario Legislature

MPP profiles, party affiliations, and Queen's Park debates from the Ontario Legislative Assembly — the same chat format as federal Hansard, for Canada's most populous province.

🔴 Senate Coverage

Senator profiles and My Senators section — see which senators represent your province and follow their activity alongside your federal MP.

🔍 System-Wide Search

Search MPs, votes, bills, and debate topics from a single screen. Results appear in sections so you can distinguish people, legislation, and debates at a glance. Also searchable from iOS Spotlight.

🎙️ Siri App Intents

"Is Parliament sitting today in epac?" and "Open an MP profile in epac" — both available via Siri and the Shortcuts app. No account required.

📊 Riding Statistics

Census data, housing market links, and electoral history for your riding — straight from Statistics Canada, CMHC, and Elections Canada. Put policy votes in the context of your community.

📣 e-Petitions

Browse and search open e-petitions from the House of Commons. Tap any petition to read the full text and sign it directly on the official Parliament of Canada site.

🔔 Follow & Notify

Follow any MP, bill, or topic. Get a local notification when a followed MP votes or speaks, when a followed bill advances, or when a new Hansard debate touches a topic you care about.

📅 Order Paper

See Parliament's schedule before the House sits — what bills, motions, and government orders are up for debate. Never miss a sitting day that matters to you.

🗳️ Election Resources

Voter registration check, polling station finder, accepted voter ID, past electoral results, and party financing disclosures — all linking to Elections Canada official sources.

🔒 Privacy First

No account. No login. Your followed MPs, bills, and topics stay on your device. Nothing is sold or shared. epac doesn't know who you are.
