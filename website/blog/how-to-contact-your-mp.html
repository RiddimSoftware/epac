How to contact your MP during an election campaign

April 27, 2026

Every sitting MP has a Hill office phone number, a constituency office address, and an official email address published on parl.ca. These contact details are public record and are updated by Parliament when an MP's office information changes.

During a campaign, MPs are often at campaign events in their riding rather than in Ottawa — Parliament is dissolved when a writ is issued, so the Hill office is inactive. Reaching out through the constituency office is more likely to receive a response during the campaign period.

When writing to your MP or a candidate, it helps to include: your name, your riding, and the specific issue, bill, or policy you are writing about. A specific question or request is more likely to receive a direct response than a general comment.

The epac app shows every MP's contact information — phone number, email address, and constituency office address — sourced directly from parl.ca. The app does not add or alter any contact details.

You can also write to any candidate running in your riding during the campaign period, even if they are not the current MP. Candidates' campaign contact information is typically published on their party's website and on their campaign websites.

Source: Parliament of Canada — parl.ca
