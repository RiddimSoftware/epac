Using epac During a Federal Election

April 27, 2026 · 4 min read

Federal elections are when your voice matters most — but they're also when disinformation, attack ads, and spin drown out the facts. epac was built for exactly this moment: to give every Canadian direct access to the verified record of what their government has actually done.

Research Your Candidates Before Voting Day

Every candidate in a federal election is trying to become an MP. If they've served before, their full voting record is in epac. Search by name in the Members tab to find:

  • Every bill they voted on — and how they voted
  • How often they voted with their party vs. independently
  • Their quarterly travel, hospitality, and office spending
  • How often they spoke in the House and on which topics
  • Who lobbied them (from the Commissioner of Lobbying registry)

For first-time candidates, use the Hansard search to find any speeches they gave at the provincial level or in parliamentary committees.

Compare Party Platforms Against the Record

Every party makes promises during an election. epac lets you check what they actually did in Parliament between elections:

  • Use the Bill Tracker to see which bills passed and which didn't
  • Check Voting Records to see how each party caucus voted on key legislation
  • Look at Expenditures to see how office budgets were used

Know Your Riding

Not sure who represents you? Enter your postal code in epac's My MP tab to instantly find your current Member of Parliament. The Riding History section links to Elections Canada for past results in your riding — so you can see how competitive it's been and how the vote has shifted over the years.

Verify Claims, Fight Disinformation

Politicians on all sides make claims during campaigns about what they've done or haven't done. epac's data comes exclusively from official sources: ourcommons.ca, Elections Canada, the Commissioner of Lobbying, and Statistics Canada. Every vote, every speech, every expenditure is sourced and verifiable.

When you share a vote from epac, the share sheet includes a link to the official openparliament.ca record. Verified facts, not screenshots.

Register and Vote

epac connects to Elections Canada's voter registration check and polling station finder. Tap the Riding Statistics section on any MP's profile for direct links. Democracy only works if you show up.

epac is free. No account required. Data from official Canadian government sources.
