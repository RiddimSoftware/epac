What is Hansard?

April 27, 2026

Hansard is the verbatim transcript of every word spoken in Canada's House of Commons, recorded by parliamentary clerks and published by Parliament. Every speech, question, answer, and procedural motion is captured verbatim — nothing is paraphrased.

The name comes from Luke Hansard, the British parliamentary printer whose family published unofficial records of UK parliamentary debates beginning in 1803. Canada has published its own official parliamentary debates since Confederation in 1867.

Each sitting day produces a transcript that is available free at parl.ca within hours of the House rising. The record is authoritative: it is what Parliament says it said.

The epac app makes Hansard searchable and readable in a conversational format. The words are the same ones Parliament published — only the presentation changes.
