How epac tracks MP voting records

April 27, 2026

The House of Commons records every formal division vote. When MPs are called to vote on a bill, a motion, or an amendment, each member's choice is recorded individually.

Parliament publishes all votes at api.open.ourcommons.ca — a free, machine-readable API maintained by the House of Commons. No third party is involved.

Each vote record includes the bill number or motion description, the date, and for every MP who participated: whether they voted Yea, Nay, Paired, or Abstained, along with the final outcome of the vote.

epac downloads these records on app launch and stores them locally using SwiftData — no intermediary server is involved. The vote history shown on any MP profile traces directly to Parliament's official published records.

Source: Parliament of Canada Open Data — api.open.ourcommons.ca
