How to look up your MP's voting record

April 27, 2026

Every formal vote in the House of Commons is published by Parliament. These recorded divisions are part of the public record and are freely available to any Canadian who wants to see them.

The record shows each MP's vote for every division: Yea, Nay, Paired, or Abstained. No vote is omitted, and the record is not curated — every MP's position on every bill, budget, and motion is included exactly as recorded by the House.

You can search by bill number, date, or MP name using Parliament's open data portal at api.open.ourcommons.ca. The data covers all recorded divisions in the current and past sessions.

The epac app displays this data directly from the Parliament of Canada open data API. No votes are filtered, summarized, or interpreted — the record shown in epac is the same record Parliament published.

To find your MP: enter your postal code in epac. The app will identify your riding and your current Member of Parliament, then show their complete voting record for the current session.

Source: Parliament of Canada — api.open.ourcommons.ca
