Press

epac is a free iOS app that makes Canada's parliamentary record readable and searchable. Every word of Hansard, every recorded vote, every MP expense, and every lobbyist meeting — sourced directly from government-published data — is available in one place. epac does not editorialize: it only shows what Parliament publishes.

Key Facts

  • PriceFree, no account required
  • PlatformsiPhone and iPad (iOS 17+)
  • Data sourcesParliament of Canada, Elections Canada, Commissioner of Lobbying
  • LanguagesEnglish and French
  • Open sourcegithub.com/RiddimSoftware/epac
  • App Storeapps.apple.com/ca/app/epac/id1224459142

Story Angles

Angle 1

Hansard in your pocket: a free app displays Parliament's verbatim debate record in a chat-style format.

Angle 2

Accountability tool: track what your MP said, how they voted, what they spent, and who lobbied them — from official government sources.

Angle 3

An app that only shows verifiable government data: no AI-generated text, no opinion, no editorial inference.

Open Source

The code is public. So is the data.

epac is open source under a public repository at github.com/RiddimSoftware/epac. Every data-fetching path traces to a documented government API. No proprietary data processing; no algorithmic ranking.
