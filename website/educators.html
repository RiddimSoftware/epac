Teach Parliament from the official record.

epac helps students follow MPs, bills, votes, debates, expenses, and lobbying activity using government-published sources. The educator kit gives instructors a ready-made guide, assignment, and data-source explainer for Canadian politics and civics courses.

What the kit includes

Instructor guide

Learning outcomes, classroom workflows, source discipline notes, and an assessment rubric.

Student assignment

A "Track Your MP for 30 Days" memo structure with evidence log and grading criteria.

Source explainer

A plain-language map of what Hansard, votes, bills, expenses, election records, and lobbying records can prove.

How to use it

Start with a riding

Students identify an MP and riding using Elections Canada or the epac app.

Collect official records

Students find one speech, one vote, and one bill, expense, or accountability record.

Write from evidence

The final memo describes what the record shows and names what the record cannot prove.

Official sources covered

  • House of Commons Hansard
  • House of Commons recorded divisions
  • Parliament of Canada LEGISinfo
  • House of Commons proactive disclosure
  • Elections Canada
  • Statistics Canada
  • Commissioner of Lobbying registry
