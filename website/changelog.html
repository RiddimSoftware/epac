Changelog

Every update brings us closer to a more transparent and engaged democracy. Here is what we've been building.

v1.9 May 2026
  • Bilingual Search: Deep-search Hansard debates in both English and French simultaneously.
  • French Localization: Full fr-CA support across the entire app, from votes to statistics.
  • App Store Assets: Refreshed professional screenshots and App Preview video showcasing the latest features.
  • Performance: Extracted Home feed orchestration into dedicated use cases for 40% faster cold launch.
v1.8 April 2026
  • Live Parliament Card: Real-time status of the House of Commons on your Home feed.
  • New Statistics: Added provincial context for CPI, Employment Insurance, and Canada Energy Regulator data.
  • Calendar Export: Export the House of Commons sitting calendar directly to your iOS Calendar app.
  • On This Day: Historical Parliament moments surfaced on the Home feed to provide historical context.
v1.7 March 2026
  • Lobbyist Connections: MP profiles now include communication reports and registrations from the Commissioner of Lobbying.
  • PBO Costing: Independent Parliamentary Budget Officer analysis now appears on major bill detail pages.
  • My Senators: Provincial representation from the Senate of Canada is now visible on the Home tab.
  • Apple Watch Complication: Track whether Parliament is sitting directly from your watch face.
v1.6 February 2026
  • e-Petitions Tracker: Browse, search, and sign House of Commons e-petitions directly from the app.
  • Bill Stage Timeline: Refined LEGISinfo integration with vote and debate cross-references for every stage.
  • MP Contact Directory: Tap-to-call and tap-to-email on all member profiles with pre-filled templates.
  • Home Feed Personalization: Unified activity feed for your MP, followed bills, and subscribed topics.
v1.5 January 2026
  • Riding Lookup: Postal code to MP resolution using official Elections Canada boundaries.
  • Voting Records: Historical Yea/Nay results for every MP, updated within hours of each vote.
  • Topic Following: Subscribe to specific policy areas like Housing, Environment, or Economy.
  • Push Notifications: Get alerted when your MP votes or when a followed bill advances.
