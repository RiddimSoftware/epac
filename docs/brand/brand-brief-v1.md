# epac Brand Brief v1

**Status:** v1 for EPAC-439  
**Decision owner:** Riddim Software  
**Last updated:** 2026-04-27

## Name

**epac** remains the product name for v1.

The name works because it is short, ownable, and already attached to the app, bundle, website, App Store listing, and repository. Treat it as a lowercase product name, not an acronym that must be explained in user-facing copy. When more context is needed, describe the category plainly: Canada's Parliament app.

**Rename decision:** no rename for v1. Revisit only if App Store search data shows the name is blocking discovery after the screenshot, subtitle, and keyword refresh work lands.

## Tagline

**Chosen:** Canada's Parliament, in your pocket.

Other candidates:

- Know what Parliament did today.
- Track your MP between elections.
- The official record, made readable.

Use the chosen tagline on broad product surfaces: App Store screenshots, website hero copy, press kit, and presentation decks. Use feature-specific headlines when the surface is focused on one workflow.

## One-Line Value Proposition

epac helps Canadians track MPs, bills, votes, debates, expenses, and lobbying activity from official government sources.

Short App Store variant:

Track your MP, bills, votes, and debates from Canada's official parliamentary record.

## Voice and Tone

epac is:

- Clear
- Neutral
- Civic
- Plain-spoken
- Sourced

epac is not:

- Snarky
- Breathless
- Partisan
- Gamified
- Viral

Write like a careful civic tool: direct verbs, concrete nouns, and visible sources. Avoid outrage framing, horse-race language, insider jargon, and claims the source data cannot prove.

## Anti-Positioning

epac is not a news app. It does not chase breaking-news framing or publish editorial takes.

epac is not a social app. It does not optimize for argument, virality, reactions, or follower graphs.

epac is not partisan. It does not rank MPs by ideology, score parties by preference, or tell users what to think.

epac is not AI-generated political commentary. Displayed app data must trace to an authoritative source, and any explanation must stay grounded in that source.

epac is not a campaign tool. It supports civic awareness and contact with representatives, not partisan organizing.

## Audience

**The accountable voter** wants to know what their MP actually does between elections, especially on votes and debates they missed.

**The policy follower** tracks specific issues such as housing, health care, climate, immigration, defence, or the economy and wants source-linked parliamentary activity.

**The civic professional** includes journalists, researchers, educators, advocates, and public servants who need fast paths to primary-source parliamentary records.

## Copy Examples

| Context | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|
| Notification | Your MP voted Yea on C-12. | Your MP just made a huge decision. |
| Empty state | No votes loaded for this member yet. | Nothing to see here. |
| Error | Could not load Hansard. Check your connection and try again. | Parliament is unavailable. |
| Source label | Source: House of Commons Votes API | Trust us. |
| Feature headline | Track a bill from introduction to Royal Assent. | Never miss political drama again. |

## Product Copy Rules

- Lead with what the user can verify: vote, speech, bill, meeting, expense, source.
- Name the institution when trust matters: House of Commons, LEGISinfo, Hansard, Parliamentary Budget Officer, Commissioner of Lobbying.
- Use "your MP" only after the user has selected a riding or postal code.
- Use "Parliament" for broad product copy and "House of Commons" when the feature specifically excludes the Senate.
- Keep government-source caveats visible where users might infer opinion, ranking, or endorsement.
