# epac App Icon v1 Explorations

## Constraints

- Match the v1 brand brief: clear, neutral, civic, plain-spoken, sourced.
- Avoid government, royal cypher, coat-of-arms, parliamentary building, and maple-leaf imagery.
- Read at small Home Screen sizes and as a 1024px App Store marketing icon.
- Provide light, dark, and iOS 18 tinted variants.

## Concepts

1. **Record Lens** (`01-record-lens.png`)

   A source document with a search lens over record lines. This connects the icon to the product promise: official records made easier to inspect.

2. **Civic Grid** (`02-civic-grid.png`)

   A neutral grid of connected modules. This suggests structured civic data, but it reads more like an enterprise dashboard than a public-facing Parliament app.

3. **Source Path** (`03-source-path.png`)

   Linked source nodes and record cards. This emphasizes traceability, but the mark is less legible at small sizes than the document-and-lens direction.

## Chosen Direction

**Record Lens** is the v1 app icon direction. It keeps the civic-data idea concrete without borrowing protected or partisan symbols: the document stands for official source material, the lens stands for inspection and search, and the green accent preserves continuity with the app's current UI. The mark is simpler than the outgoing Parliament silhouette, scales down cleanly, and supports light, dark, tinted, and marketing outputs from the same geometry.
