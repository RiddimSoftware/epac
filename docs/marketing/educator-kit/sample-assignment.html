Sample Assignment: Track Your MP for 30 Days

Purpose

This assignment asks you to follow one Member of Parliament using official public records. Your goal is not to praise or criticize the MP. Your goal is to describe what the record shows and explain why it matters for representation.

Instructions

  1. Identify your federal riding and MP using Elections Canada or the epac app.
  2. Follow that MP for 30 days.
  3. Collect at least three official records: one Hansard speech, question, or answer; one recorded vote; and one bill interaction, expenditure item, or other official accountability record.
  4. For each record, save the source, date, title, and a short note on why it is relevant.
  5. Write a 900 to 1,200 word memo answering: What did this MP do in Parliament during the period you tracked?

Memo structure

  • Introduction: name the MP, riding, party, and tracking period.
  • Evidence summary: describe the three records in plain language.
  • Analysis: explain what the records show about representation, policy focus, or parliamentary role.
  • Limits: explain what your evidence cannot prove.
  • Source list: cite official records.
