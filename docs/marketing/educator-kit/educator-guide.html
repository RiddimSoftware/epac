epac educator kit

Educator Guide

Audience: Canadian politics, public policy, civics, journalism, and social studies instructors.

epac is a free iOS app that helps students follow Parliament using official public records: Hansard, recorded votes, bills, MP profiles, expenditures, Elections Canada data, Statistics Canada references, and lobbying registry records.

Learning outcomes

  • Identify a Member of Parliament and riding using an official source.
  • Read Hansard as a primary source.
  • Trace a federal bill through parliamentary stages.
  • Compare an MP's recorded votes with party, riding, and issue context.
  • Cite official parliamentary and government sources in analysis.
  • Explain the limits of what government records can and cannot show.

Suggested course uses

One class: Students find their MP, one recent vote, and one recent speech, then compare whether the vote and speech address the same policy area.

Two weeks: Assign "Track Your MP for 30 Days." Students collect a speech, a recorded vote, and either an expense disclosure or bill interaction.

Semester: Students maintain a weekly source log for a riding or topic.

Classroom workflow

  1. Ask students to install epac or pair with a classmate who can access iOS.
  2. Review key terms on the FAQ page at epac.riddimsoftware.com/faq.html.
  3. Have students identify a riding and MP.
  4. Require source links for each factual claim.
  5. Grade the evidence trail, not political agreement.

Instructor checklist

  • Confirm the assignment fits course privacy and accessibility policy.
  • Provide a non-iOS alternative for students who need it.
  • Require source links or screenshots of official records.
  • Remind students that epac is independent and not affiliated with government.
