Data Sources Explainer

epac only displays data that traces to official public sources. It is not a news app, a commentary app, or an AI-generated summary product.

How students should cite epac work

  • Use epac to find the record, then cite the official source behind it.
  • Use dates, institution names, and source URLs wherever possible.
  • Screenshots help with review, but source URLs are stronger evidence.

What epac does not claim

epac does not rank MPs by ideology, score parties by preference, infer motive, summarize debates with generative AI, or decide whether a political statement is true.

Verification exercise

  1. Pick a speech, vote, bill, or expense item.
  2. Identify the institution that published it.
  3. Locate the original record.
  4. Write two sentences explaining what the record proves.
  5. Write one sentence explaining what the record does not prove.
