# Product Hunt Launch Plan

Status: ready for manual launch execution

Ticket: EPAC-226

Owner: Riddim Software

This plan prepares the epac Product Hunt launch without asking for direct upvotes. Product Hunt's current guidance allows makers to share a launch link, but asks makers not to directly ask people to upvote. Use the outreach copy below to ask people to visit, try the app, and leave a useful comment.

## Source Rules

- Product Hunt launch guide: launch at 12:01 AM Pacific when planning ahead.
- Product Hunt posting docs: a personal account posts the product; company accounts cannot post.
- Product Hunt preparation docs: tagline maximum is 60 characters.
- Product Hunt preparation docs: choose up to 3 launch tags.
- Product Hunt preparation docs: the primary product URL should not use tracking parameters or shortened links.
- Product Hunt posting docs: gallery images should be 1270 x 760 and the post needs at least two images before the gallery appears.
- Product Hunt posting docs: thumbnail should be square.
- Product Hunt preparation docs: images and GIFs must be under 3 MB.
- Product Hunt posting docs: videos must be YouTube URLs and should be uploaded before launch.
- epac brand brief: use neutral, sourced, civic copy. Do not use partisan, breathless, or viral language.

## Maker First Comment

```text
Hi Product Hunt - I built epac because Canada's parliamentary record is public, but it is still hard to follow on a phone.

epac turns official Canadian government data into a practical iPhone app: Hansard debates, MP profiles, recorded votes, bills, expenses, lobbying activity, and source links.

The product rule is simple: no commentary, no partisan scoring, and no AI-generated political summaries. If epac shows a claim, it traces back to an authoritative source such as Hansard, the House of Commons, LEGISinfo, the Commissioner of Lobbying, or other published government records.

The goal is to help Canadians answer everyday civic questions:

- What did my MP vote for?
- What did Parliament debate today?
- Where is this bill in the process?
- Who is lobbying public office holders?
- How can I contact my representative?

I'd be grateful for feedback on clarity, trust, and whether the app makes the official record easier to use.
```

## Trade-Offs

- Do not directly ask for upvotes. Product Hunt's current guidance permits sharing the launch link, but says not to ask people directly to upvote.
- Do not use political urgency framing. The brand is verified civic utility, not outrage or breaking-news commentary.
- Do not include unsourced facts in launch copy. Product Hunt copy should describe the product; app content remains tied to official sources.
