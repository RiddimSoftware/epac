## One-line pitch

epac makes Canada's Parliament readable: Hansard debates, MP votes, expenses, and lobbyist meetings — from official government sources only, free.

## Why now (election angle)

During a federal election campaign, Canadians search for: their MP's voting record, riding information, party platform analysis, and how to contact their representative. epac answers all four — with data sourced directly from Parliament, not commentary. An editorial feature during election season positions epac as the definitive civic resource for the 35 million Canadians eligible to vote.

## What the app does

- **Hansard debates** in a readable chat format — every word spoken in Parliament since the current session
- **MP voting records** from Parliament's open data API — every recorded division, with Yea/Nay/Paired
- **MP expenses** from proactive disclosure — travel, hospitality, and contracts
- **Lobbyist connections** from the Commissioner of Lobbying registry
- **Bill tracker** for every bill in the current Parliament — introduction to Royal Assent
- **Who is my MP?** — postal code lookup links users to their representative
- **App Clip** — "Who is my MP?" in under 30 seconds, no install required

## Data philosophy

Every fact in epac traces to a government-published source. No AI-generated text. No editorial inference. No opinion. The code and the data are both public.

## App Clip angle

The epac App Clip ("Who is my MP?") is ideal for election-season editorial: it works on Safari Smart Banners, QR codes at polling places, and campaign posters. Users can find their MP without installing anything.
