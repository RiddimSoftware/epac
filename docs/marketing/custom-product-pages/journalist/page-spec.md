# Custom Product Page: Journalists & Researchers

**App Store Connect page name:** journalist-researcher
**Target URL slug:** (assigned by ASC after creation)
**Audience:** Canadian political journalists, policy researchers, parliamentary press gallery

## Positioning

"The parliamentary record. Searchable, citable, and always sourced."

Journalists need citations they can publish. epac's hard constraint — only verified government data — is a feature, not a limitation. Every data point links to its source.

## How to create in App Store Connect

1. App Store Connect → My Apps → epac → App Store tab → Product Pages
2. Click + to create new custom page
3. Name: "journalist-researcher"
4. Upload the 6 screenshots
5. Add the description and promotional text variants
6. Click Submit
7. Copy the resulting ppid URL parameter for use in press outreach (EPAC-418)
