## Quarterly KPI review agenda (add to Retrospective)

1. Update the Supporting KPIs table with current numbers
2. Is the primary KPI (monthly installs) on the growth curve?
3. Which channel is over-performing? Double down on it.
4. Which channel is under-performing? Diagnose or deprioritize.
5. Revise month-6 and month-12 targets if baseline understanding has changed significantly.
6. Is conversion rate ≥ 3%? If not, screenshot refresh (EPAC-109) is the highest-leverage action.

## What good looks like at 12 months

- **Monthly installs**: 20× the April 2026 baseline
- **Rating**: ≥ 4.5 stars on ≥ 100 ratings
- **D28 retention**: ≥ 15% — users return to check Parliament at least monthly
- **Search Console**: epac.ca appears in top 10 results for "[riding name] MP" queries
- **App Store Search**: organic impressions growing without paid spend
- **Media mentions**: At least 4 per year (one per quarter), including at least one national outlet
