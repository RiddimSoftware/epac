## Outreach Email Template

Subject: Free source-based Parliament app for Canadian politics and civics classes

Hello,

I am reaching out from epac, a free iOS app that helps Canadians follow MPs, bills, votes, debates, expenses, and lobbying activity from official government sources. It is independent, open source, and does not require an account.

We created a small educator kit for Canadian politics, public policy, journalism, and civics courses: an instructor guide, a data-sources explainer, and a sample "Track Your MP for 30 Days" assignment. The materials are designed around primary-source records from Parliament of Canada, Elections Canada, Statistics Canada, LEGISinfo, and related official sources.

The kit is available at:
https://epac.riddimsoftware.com/educators.html

If it is useful for your instructors or students, please feel free to circulate it. I would also welcome feedback on what would make the resource more useful in a course setting.

Best,
Sunny Purewal
Riddim Software
