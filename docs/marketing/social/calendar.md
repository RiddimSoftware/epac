# Social media content calendar

Authoritative, sourced, no commentary. Every post links to either the official
record (Hansard, Parliament of Canada, Office of the Auditor General, etc.) or
to a deep-link into the epac app that itself shows verified data from those
sources. We don't editorialize, we don't speculate, we don't take sides.

This calendar is the playbook for a one-person social channel that scales with
Parliament's own schedule.

## Posting cadence

The cadence is driven by Parliament, not by an editorial calendar.

| Trigger | Cadence | Latency |
|---|---|---|
| Parliament sitting day | 1× per sitting morning | Post by 09:30 ET |
| Major recorded vote | 1× per vote, only when stakes are high (third reading, opposition motion, supply day) | Within 60 min of vote |
| New Hansard available | 1× when the day's transcript is indexed in epac | Same day |
| Federal Budget tabled | 1× per Budget Day | Within 30 min of tabling |
| Auditor General report | 1× per report | Day of release |
| New TestFlight / App Store version | 1× per public release | At release time |

A quiet day (no sitting, no vote, no report) is a quiet day. Don't post for
the sake of posting.

## Post archetypes

Every post fits one of these. Variants live in the launch posts below.

### A. Sitting-day agenda

> Parliament is sitting today. On the agenda: {subject 1}, {subject 2},
> {subject 3}. Order Paper: https://www.parl.ca/legisinfo/

### B. Vote result

> {Passed|Defeated} {Yea}–{Nay}: {Bill number} — {bill short title}.
> See how MPs in {your province} voted: {epac universal link}

### C. New Hansard available

> Hansard for {Date} is available. Most-discussed today: {subject}.
> Read the verbatim record in epac: {epac universal link}

### D. Budget Day

> The federal budget was tabled today. Three commitments to watch:
> 1. {commitment} — {dollar amount}, {timing}
> 2. {commitment} — {dollar amount}, {timing}
> 3. {commitment} — {dollar amount}, {timing}
> Source: budget.canada.ca

### E. Auditor General report

> The Auditor General released a new audit of {program}.
> Key finding: "{single sentence quoted from the report}"
> Full report: {oag-bvg.gc.ca link}

### F. Product release

> Out today: epac {version}. {one-line user-facing change from the
> Release-Note line of the merged PRs}.
> Free on iPhone: https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/epac/id...

## Voice rules

- Sourced — every claim links to the official record.
- No opinion words: "wrong", "good", "bad", "right", "should".
- No editorial framings: "shocking", "unprecedented", "historic" (unless the
  source explicitly uses the word).
- No partisan framings: don't frame a vote as a win/loss for any party.
- Numbers are quoted verbatim from the source — never rounded or restated.
- Hashtags: `#cdnpoli` only when the post would benefit from being indexed in
  the Canadian politics community feed. No more than one tag per post.
- Bluesky vs X: identical text. Don't tune posts per platform.
- 280 character limit on X is the binding constraint — write every post to
  that limit so the same string ships to Bluesky.

## What we don't post

- Anything generated by an LLM. The brand is verified facts, end of.
  ([memory: feedback_no_llm_content].)
- Anything that frames a person — MP, senator, witness, journalist — as a
  villain or a hero.
- Memes, opinion polls, "hot takes", reactions to other accounts.
- Replies to political commentary unless to correct a factual error with a
  single source link.
- Engagement bait ("retweet if…", "what do you think?").

## Launch posts (first 10 drafts)

These are draft posts to publish in order during the first week the accounts
are live. Each is sourced and within 280 characters. Replace `{epac
universal link}` and `{App Store URL}` once the production deep-link
infrastructure (EPAC-214 already merged) and the App Store listing are
finalized.

1. **Account intro**

   > Canada's Parliament, in your pocket. Verbatim Hansard, voting records,
   > MP expenses — all from official sources. No commentary, no spin.
   > Free: {App Store URL}

2. **Why we exist** (no opinion, just frame)

   > Hansard is the official verbatim record of every word spoken in the
   > House of Commons. epac displays it as a chat. The text is exactly what
   > Parliament publishes — we just make it readable on a phone.

3. **Vote tracking**

   > Every recorded vote in the House is published by Parliament with each
   > MP listed as Yea or Nay. epac shows you how *your* MP voted, by
   > postal code. Source: ourcommons.ca votes.

4. **MP expenditures**

   > MP office expenses are public. Members' Expenditures Reports are
   > released quarterly by the House of Commons. epac surfaces them by MP
   > and category. Source: ourcommons.ca/proactivedisclosure

5. **Today's sitting** (template — substitute live data when posting)

   > Parliament is sitting today. On the agenda: Question Period at 14:15,
   > Government Orders, two opposition-day debates. Order Paper:
   > parl.ca/legisinfo

6. **Bill tracker**

   > Following a federal bill is hard — it moves between chambers, gets
   > amended, sometimes prorogued. epac tracks every government and
   > private member's bill from first reading to royal assent. Source:
   > LEGISinfo.

7. **Budget commitments** (template — substitute commitments)

   > The federal budget was tabled today. Three commitments to watch:
   > {commitment 1}, {commitment 2}, {commitment 3}. Full text:
   > budget.canada.ca

8. **Auditor General** (template — substitute audit)

   > The AG released a new audit today: "{audit title}". Key finding:
   > "{quoted sentence from report}". Full report: oag-bvg.gc.ca

9. **Senate work**

   > The Senate of Canada conducts standing committee studies that produce
   > some of the most expert testimony in the public record. epac surfaces
   > Senate proceedings alongside House work. Source: sencanada.ca

10. **What's not in the app** (transparency post)

    > epac doesn't include LLM-generated summaries, opinion takes, or
    > inferred party positions. Everything you see traces to an
    > authoritative source — Hansard, ourcommons.ca, budget.canada.ca,
    > oag-bvg.gc.ca, statcan.gc.ca. That's the brand.

## After launch

When the first 10 posts have shipped, future posts follow the cadence and
archetypes above. There is no "growth hacking" here — the audience grows
because the content is reliably useful to people who follow Canadian
politics professionally.

If a post would require subjective judgment ("was this vote close?",
"was this audit damning?"), don't post.

## Future automation

Several archetypes (B, C, F) can be automated from backend signals:

- **Vote result** — when `votes.ourcommons.ca` indexes a new third-reading
  vote, the backend can post archetype B with the bill number, vote
  margin, and a generated `epac://vote/{id}` deep link.
- **New Hansard available** — when the daily-fetch pipeline indexes a new
  sitting, post archetype C.
- **Product release** — when the release pipeline writes
  `ios/fastlane/metadata/en-CA/release_notes.txt`, post archetype F.

Automation is out of scope for EPAC-138 — manual posting first; automation
is a follow-up ticket once the cadence is proven.
