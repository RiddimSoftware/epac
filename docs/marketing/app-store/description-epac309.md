## Full description (after "More" — up to 4000 characters)

```
Parliament voted last night. Did you know how your MP voted?

epac shows you every speech, vote, expense, and lobbying meeting from Canada's official government record — the Hansard, the Parliamentary voting API, the Registry of Lobbyists. See what your elected representative is actually doing between elections.

Free. No ads. No AI-generated content. Only verified government data.

——

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH EPAC

📋 DEBATES
Read House of Commons debates in a format you can actually follow — the official Hansard verbatim transcript as a conversation, not a wall of text. Filter by MP, topic, or date. Search across every sitting day.

🗳️ VOTING RECORDS
See how every MP voted on every recorded division. Compare their speeches on housing to how they voted on housing bills. The accountability gap, made visible. Every vote is sourced from the House of Commons voting API.

📍 MY MP
Enter your postal code. Get your federal representative. See everything they've said, voted on, spent, and been lobbied about — in one personalized feed. Follows them automatically as Parliament sits.

📜 BILLS TRACKER
Track any bill from introduction to Royal Assent. See the stage it's at, the Parliamentary Budget Office cost estimate, every vote it triggered, and the full debate Hansard. Follow a bill and get notified when it advances.

💰 EXPENDITURES
See how your MP spends their office budget — travel, hospitality, contracts, and staff — every quarter, from the official parliamentary disclosure. Searchable and sortable across all 338 MPs.

🏛️ LOBBYIST CONNECTIONS
See which lobbyists have met with your MP and on what topics, sourced from the federal Registry of Lobbyists. Connects the dots between corporate interests and votes.

🔔 NOTIFICATIONS
Follow any MP, bill, or policy topic. Get a notification when they speak in Parliament, when a bill advances, or when a subscribed topic is debated. All notifications link directly to the source transcript.

——

DATA SOURCES

epac uses only verified, authoritative Canadian government data:
• House of Commons Hansard (debates and speeches)
• House of Commons Votes (every recorded division)
• Parliament of Canada members database
• LEGISinfo (bills and legislative history)
• Parliamentary Budget Office cost estimates
• Government of Canada proactive disclosure (MP expenditures)
• Office of the Commissioner of Lobbying (Registry of Lobbyists)
• Senate of Canada (senators and committee proceedings)

No editorial content. No AI-generated summaries. No opinion. Only the parliamentary record.

——

PRIVACY

epac requires no account, no email, and no login. Your followed MPs, bills, and topics are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server. No tracking. No analytics. No ads.

Built by Riddim Software, an independent Canadian developer.
```
