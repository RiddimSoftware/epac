Legislative Meeting Archive

Looking for a series of committee meetings? Documents from a particular period in the Legislative Assembly?

Select the type of meeting you are looking for, then select a date range. Once you click "Filter", the search will display a list of dates and the publications available for each date.

Our archives are not complete, but we are working to add new content all the time. Digitized files for the Debates (Hansard) go back to 1947, but most other documents are available beginning in the late 1990s or early 2000s.

As always, if you have any questions, we would be happy to help.
