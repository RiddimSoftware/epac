epac brings Canada's Parliament to your pocket. Follow every debate, track every vote, and hold your government accountable — all from authoritative government sources, updated the same day Parliament sits.

**YOUR MP AT A GLANCE**
See exactly how your Member of Parliament votes on every bill. Read their full debate history, written questions, and interventions. Contact them directly from their profile.

**HANSARD DEBATES IN PLAIN FORMAT**
The House of Commons Hansard — Canada's official parliamentary record — presented as a readable conversation. Follow any debate topic and get notified when Parliament discusses issues you care about: housing, healthcare, climate, the economy, and more.

**BILLS TRACKER**
Every bill currently before Parliament, with its status, reading history, and voting record. Search by keyword or topic. Share any bill with a verified government source link.

**ACCOUNTABILITY TOOLS**
• MP Expenditures — travel, hospitality, and office budgets for every member
• Written Questions — see what MPs are asking the government in writing
• Petitions — browse House of Commons e-petitions and government responses
• Committee Transcripts — standing committee hearings and evidence

**BEYOND OTTAWA**
• Ontario Legislature — MPP profiles and Queen's Park debates
• Senate — Senator profiles and the Senate as a second chamber
• Election Resources — voter registration, polling stations, and Elections Canada links

**FOLLOW WHAT MATTERS**
During onboarding, pick the topics you care about. epac notifies you the same evening Parliament debates them — directly from Hansard, within hours of publication.

**SIRI & SPOTLIGHT**
Ask Siri "Is Parliament sitting today?" or search any MP by name from iOS Spotlight. Deep links open directly to any debate, bill, or member profile from Safari or Messages.

**DATA YOU CAN TRUST**
Every piece of information in epac traces directly to an authoritative government source — Parliament.ca, the Legislative Assembly of Ontario, or Elections Canada. No AI-generated content. No opinion. No ads. No tracking.
